About DATA DOUBLE

DATA DOUBLE is a fast paced decision making game based on cybersecurity. A powerful Artifical Intelligence, DAVE, has overthrown your system, using the patel proy firewall, your objective is to take down Dave, by identifying between safe and fraudalent scenarios. Dave has powerful abilities, including being able to access your computer, which you must control to stop him from taking control of your system

How to Play

  • Read through every scenario given to you, determine whether it is safe or fraudalent
  • Making the wrong choice increases Dave's Intelligence
  • Once Dave's Intelligence reaches the maximum level, he will attack you and then the heartbeat game will commence
  • Upon surviving for 3 minutes, you will then defeat Dave to win the game.
