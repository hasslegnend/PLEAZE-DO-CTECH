About the Game

Trapped in a nightmare of blinking phishing lures and glowing spam, Robbie never asked to be here. One wrong click, and the internet turned against him.

Now every question is life or death. Answer right, and you strike back hard. Answer wrong, and watch him grow stronger, angrier, hungrier for your data.

Retro pixels. Pounding chiptunes. Real cybersecurity knowledge disguised as chaos.

The Phisherman is waiting. Do you have what it takes to survive?

How to Play

  • Move Robbie left and right to dodge phishing lures and spam while the fight is happening
  • A cybersecurity question appears on screen mid-battle, keep moving while you read it
  • Choose the correct answer to strike back and deal damage
  • Wrong answers make the boss stronger, so think fast without stopping
  • Survive the chaos and defeat the Phisherman before he overwhelms you

Real Cyber Lessons

Every question tests real cybersecurity knowledge, phishing, passwords, and malware.

Retro Chiptune OST

Pixel art visuals and pounding chiptune music keep every fight intense.

One Boss, Rising Difficulty

The Phisherman gets faster and angrier the longer the fight goes on.
