GRAND CYBER SECURITY

Can you protect your money from online scams?

Enter the World of Cyber Security

Grand Cyber Security is a decision-making game where you must protect your money from scammers.

Throughout the game, you will face different situations that could be safe, suspicious or dangerous.

Think carefully before making a decision because one wrong choice could cost you your money.

Your Mission

Your goal is to survive the different situations while keeping as much money as possible.

  • Read the situation carefully.
  • Look for signs of a scam.
  • Think about the safest choice.
  • Protect your money and personal information.

What You Will Learn

SPOT SCAMS

Learn how to recognise suspicious messages, offers and situations.

MAKE DECISIONS

Choose what you think is the safest option in each situation.

PROTECT YOUR MONEY

Try to finish the game without losing your money to scammers.

Are You Ready?

Stay alert, think before you click and protect yourself from cyber scams.
