OUR BUNDLES

The Class Anthology contains 3 unique cyber games that provide a fun experience and enlighten the player with new cyber security skills.

The Class Anthology includes:

  • Cyber Bossfight, by Adyansh Jha
  • Play a Bullet Hell Cyber game where you face bosses with a unique twist,
  • having a fun time and also learning important cyber skills!
  • Grand Cyber Security, by Emad Ilyas
  • A Virtual Simulation game where you must spend your money wisely, and
  • avoid scammers who lurk every corner by making the correct choices!
  • Data Double, by Hassan Noori
  • An educational but also very fun cybersecurity awareness game which is
  • fast paced, your goal is to defend yourself against a rogue AI!
